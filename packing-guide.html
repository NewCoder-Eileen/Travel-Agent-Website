Perfect for: Kenya, Tanzania, Botswana, South Africa, Zimbabwe, Rwanda (gorilla trekking)

👕 Safari Clothing (Neutral Tones ONLY)
🔭 Game Drive Essentials
🦟 Health & Protection
🧳 Luggage Notes
💡 Marcus's Pro Tip: Most luxury safari camps offer same-day laundry service, so you really only need 3–4 days of clothing. The critical rules: neutral tones, no white, long sleeves for insect protection, and ALWAYS bring a fleece — dawn game drives in the Masai Mara can be surprisingly cold.

Winter & Cold-Weather Destinations

Perfect for: Iceland, Norway, Canada, Japan (ski), Swiss Alps, Lapland, Alaska

🧥 Cold-Weather Clothing
🎿 Winter Activities
💡 Northern Lights Tips
💊 Health & Comfort
💡 Agent Tip: The layering system is everything in cold destinations: moisture-wicking base, insulating mid-layer, and weatherproof outer shell. This lets you easily regulate temperature as you move between heated interiors and the cold outside. Never wear cotton as a base layer — it holds moisture and can cause hypothermia.
