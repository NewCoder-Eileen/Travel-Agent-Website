Cancellations & Refunds

Our standard cancellation schedule (from departure date):

  • 90+ days before departure: Full deposit refund, minus a $150 administrative fee
  • 60–89 days: 50% refund of total booking value
  • 30–59 days: 25% refund of total booking value
  • Under 30 days: Non-refundable (we strongly recommend travel insurance to cover this)

Please note that some suppliers (particularly in peak season or for specialty accommodations) may apply their own stricter cancellation terms, which your agent will disclose upfront.

💡 Comprehensive travel insurance covers cancellation for most covered reasons (medical emergencies, redundancy, bereavement). We strongly recommend purchasing this at the time of booking.

Visas & Entry Requirements

Visa requirements depend on your passport nationality and your destination. Your WanderLux agent will check and advise on exact requirements at the time of booking. Some common examples:

  • Maldives: Visa on arrival (free) for all nationalities — 30 days
  • Thailand: Visa-free for most Western passports (30–60 days)
  • Japan: Visa-free for most Western passports (90 days)
  • Kenya: e-Visa required (apply online, ~$51 USD)
  • India: e-Visa required (apply online, $25–$80 depending on nationality)
  • Schengen Zone (Europe): Visa-free for US/UK/Australian passports (90 days)
💡 Always ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months beyond your return date. If not, renew it before booking.

Some destinations require proof of vaccination as a condition of entry:

  • Yellow Fever Certificate: Required for entry into many African countries (Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, etc.) if you've transited through a Yellow Fever zone
  • Malaria prophylaxis: Strongly recommended (not required for entry) for Sub-Saharan Africa, Southeast Asia, and parts of South America
  • General recommendations: We advise all travelers to be up to date on Hepatitis A/B, Typhoid, Tetanus, and routine vaccinations

Visit your doctor or travel health clinic at least 6–8 weeks before departure. Your WanderLux agent will provide destination-specific health advisories with every itinerary.

We monitor all destinations daily against US State Department, UK Foreign Office, and Australian DFAT travel advisories. We do not book travel to destinations rated "Level 4: Do Not Travel."

If an advisory is issued for your booked destination after you've confirmed, we will always contact you, assess the situation, and offer alternatives or a full refund if the destination becomes unsafe.

Travel Tips & Best Practices

Here are quick guides for our most-requested destinations:

  • Maldives: Nov–April (dry season, calm seas) — avoid May–October (monsoon season)
  • Bali: May–September (dry season) — July–August can be crowded, June/September offer the best balance
  • Japan: March–April (cherry blossom) or October–November (autumn foliage) — avoid Golden Week (late April/early May)
  • Kenya Safari: July–October (Great Migration) or January–February (calving season)
  • Iceland: June–August (midnight sun + puffins) or November–February (Northern Lights)
  • Santorini: May–June or September–October — avoid July–August peak when it's extremely crowded and expensive
  • Paris: April–June (spring) or September–October (autumn) — avoid August (locals leave, tourists flood in)

Our agents' top packing tips:

  • Roll clothes instead of folding — saves 30% more space
  • Use packing cubes to stay organized and find things instantly
  • Pack a change of clothes and one day's medication in your carry-on (in case checked luggage is delayed)
  • Photograph your luggage contents before departure for insurance claims
  • Take digital copies of all documents and email them to yourself
  • For safari: 15kg max on light aircraft — soft-sided bags only

Jet lag is manageable with the right strategy:

  • Shift your sleep schedule 2–3 days before departure — move bedtime 1 hour toward your destination time zone each night
  • Stay hydrated — cabin air is extremely dry. Drink 200ml of water per hour of flight
  • Avoid alcohol on the flight — it dehydrates you and disrupts sleep quality
  • Take melatonin (0.5–3mg) 30 minutes before your target bedtime in the new time zone
  • Get sunlight immediately on arrival — natural light is the most powerful circadian rhythm reset
  • Stay awake until local bedtime on your first day — resist the nap
💡 For eastward travel (typically harder), avoid flights that arrive in the morning. If possible, choose afternoon arrivals so you only need to stay up a few more hours.
